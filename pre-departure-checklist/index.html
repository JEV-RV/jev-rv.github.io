⚠️ Driving licence & weight: Standard B licence officially covers vehicles up to 3,500 kg GVWR. Most EU countries apply tourist tolerance for privately-used motorhomes above this — but Germany and Austria may require C1 (3,500–7,500 kg) or C (>7,500 kg). Insurance may be voided in an accident if your licence category does not match your vehicle weight.
